================================================================================
 TRADING SIGNALS EXPLAINED
================================================================================

SIGNAL TYPES:
BUY CE   - Strong call interest (bullish momentum)
SELL CE  - Call resistance level (defensive position)
BUY PE   - Put support level (defensive play)
SELL PE  - Put accumulation (bearish indicator)

SENTIMENT ANALYSIS:
PCR > 1.3   - BULLISH (more puts than calls)
PCR < 0.9   - BEARISH (more calls than puts)
0.9 - 1.3   - NEUTRAL (balanced market)

STRATEGIES PROVIDED:
Bullish:   Buy ATM Call, Bull Call Spread, Sell OTM Put
Bearish:   Buy ATM Put, Bear Put Spread, Sell OTM Call
Neutral:   Short Straddle, Iron Condor, Long Straddle
